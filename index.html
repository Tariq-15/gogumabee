#about me

the hand behind the pencil

I'm Sarang, the human half of goguma_bee. I've filled the margins of every notebook I've owned with faces, and now I get to share them with over sixteen thousand wonderful people.

I like drawing people the way the ones who love them see them: soft light, a little blush, tiny doodles tucked into the corners. Most pieces start as graphite on real paper. Some stay that way, and some get coloured in digitally.

#faq

things people usually ask

How long does a commission take?

Usually 1–3 weeks, depending on the queue and the piece. Need it sooner? Rush is +40% if I can fit it in.

How do I pay?

50% upfront and 50% before the final file. bKash / Nagad for local clients. International clients, just ask and we'll find what works.

What references should I send?

Clear, well-lit photos (a couple of angles help), plus any outfit, pose or vibe notes. A screenshot of a pose you love is great too.

Can I use the art as my pfp, or print it?

Yes, for personal use! Please credit @goguma_bee when you post it. For merch, ads or anything you sell, ask me about a commercial licence first.

Can I get the original pencil drawing?

Sometimes! Ask in your DM and I'll let you know if shipping works for where you are.

What about refunds?

Full refund before I start sketching, 50% after the sketch stage, and none once the final piece is delivered.
