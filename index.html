#how it works

from your idea to your inbox

  1. 1

    say hi

    Fill in the form below or DM @goguma_bee with your idea and reference photos.

  2. 2

    get a quote

    I'll confirm the price and timeline, then save a slot for you.

  3. 3

    pay half

    50% upfront so I can start. The rest is due before the final file.

  4. 4

    sketch check

    You see a rough sketch first. Small changes here are free.

  5. 5

    it's yours!

    The finished high-res piece lands in your DMs or email, ready to post or print.
